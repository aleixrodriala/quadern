Fourth beta.
- Record with a headset or another microphone: when one is connected, the recorder shows which microphone is recording. Tap it to switch, even in the middle of a note. Your choice is used again whenever it's connected.
- Music pauses while you record, and picks up again when you stop.
- Fixed on Android 10 to 12: notes weren't finished or transcribed after you stopped recording. The audio was kept, and those notes finish when you open this version.
